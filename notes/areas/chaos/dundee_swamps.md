# Dundee swamps

## Swamp 1

Need to talk to park ranger and pledge to dundee poachers.

Allows you to give animal bits to hunter in the east.  Not sure what this is 
for.

Once you have 25k reps, you can go into hunters camp.

## Camp

30k reps

### Cure mini quest

- talk to nurse to get first quest
    - talk to medic to solve it (toad glands from swamp 1, need 5)
- give cure to nurse
- say hi to patient
- talk to mechanic

### Necklace mini quest

- give 15-20 tooth and/or fangs to mechanic (from swamp 1)

### Master hunter mini quest

- tell master hunter 'dilligaf', find alpha croc
- say alpha
- say yes
|Hunter says: Ok then, but don't say I didn't warn you. Search the
|             outskirts of the swamp for an entrance to a cave, you will
|             find your alpha there. Bring me its pelt if you successful.
- 'ls cliff' along rim of swamp 1
- 'crawl into opening'
- have to kill until it drops a pelt

After can go into swamp 2
